About Us

LaborTrack is dedicated to empowering workers by providing tools and resources to understand and advocate for their labor rights.
Our platform offers a wage calculator, secure violation reporting, and a comprehensive labor rights library to support fair labor practices worldwide.

Our Mission

Our mission is to promote fair labor practices and empower workers with the knowledge and tools they need to advocate for their rights.
We strive to create a safer and more equitable work environment for all.

Our Vision

We envision a world where every worker is informed, empowered, and able to exercise their labor rights without fear of retaliation.
Through technology and education, we aim to foster a global community that values and upholds the dignity of work.

Our Values

At LaborTrack, we are committed to integrity, transparency, and respect for all workers.
We believe in the power of education and technology to drive positive change in the labor landscape.
Our values guide us in our mission to support and advocate for workers' rights worldwide.
